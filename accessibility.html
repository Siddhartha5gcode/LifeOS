Commitment to Digital Accessibility

LifeOS & Sync is dedicated to providing an accessible web experience for users of all abilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 Level AA) standards.

Accessibility Features

  • High Contrast Visuals: High-contrast color palette (Forest Green `#163A24` on Cream `#FBF7EE` background) ensuring optimal text readability.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Interactive elements including forms, workspace tab buttons, and blog triggers are accessible via standard keyboard navigation.
