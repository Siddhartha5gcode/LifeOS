1. Information We Collect

LifeOS & Sync respects your personal privacy. When registering or using our platform, we collect minimal operational information required to synchronize your personal assistant state and publish community blog articles:

  • Account Credentials: Name, Gmail address, Mobile number (with country code), and encrypted password stored securely on our Render cloud authentication server.
  • Personal Assistant Data: Daily milk delivery logs, expense categories, loan records, habit logs, and journal notes.
  • Public Blog Contributions: Article titles, categories, stories, likes, and public comments.

2. How We Store Your Data

User account credentials and sensitive tracking data are never stored locally in plain-text local browser storage. All authentication and data backups execute against our dedicated Render Express REST API and Google Drive cloud servers.

3. Public vs Private Isolation

We enforce strict boundary separation between your Personal Workspace and Public Blog Platform. Personal tracking data (milk delivery records, personal finances, loan ledgers) is private to your authenticated account and is never accessible to public visitors.
